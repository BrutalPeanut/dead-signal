## Full description (4000)
One courier. One thumb. A thousand zombies.

The city went dark at 03:14 AM. You're the last courier still carrying the signal, and every sector between you and the relay is crawling with the dead. Move with one thumb — your guns aim and fire on their own. All you have to do is survive.

⚡ EASY TO PLAY, HARD TO PUT DOWN
• One-thumb controls: drag to move, the auto-aim does the shooting
• Short, punchy runs that fit a coffee break
• Every run is different: pick upgrades as you level up and build your perfect loadout

🔥 EVOLVE YOUR GUNS
• Collect weapons and passive skills during each run
• Pair the right skills to evolve guns into devastating super-weapons
• Flamethrowers, miniguns, rocket launchers, chain lightning, spinning saw blades and more

🧟 THE HORDE NEVER STOPS
• Hundreds of zombies on screen at once
• Elite mutants, swarms, and giant bosses that will test every build
• 20 campaign sectors across a dead downtown, biolabs, a spaceport and a melting reactor

🛡 GET STRONGER EVERY RUN
• Gear up with gun mods, armor and gadgets, then merge duplicates into rarer gear
• Talents, account levels and Ascension keep your progress growing
• Daily Challenge with its own leaderboard, plus Endless mode: how long can you last?

🎁 FAIR AND FREE
• Free to play, playable offline after the first launch
• Ads are optional: watch one only when you want a bonus reward, and never mid-fight
• Your progress backs up with a cloud save code
