Devlog

What's new in Dead Signal, newest first

Dead Signal is made by one developer and updated constantly. Here's what has changed recently, written for players. If something feels off, email the address at the bottom of the page. Many of these changes started as player feedback.

November 15, 2026 · Founders and a fairer web version

  • You're a Founder. Everyone who plays before the full launch is a Founder. Your progress carries straight into the release, and Founders get a one-time Founder's Pack (5 crate keys, 2,500 gold and 150 scrap) at launch, plus a gold star on their lobby avatar.
  • Safer saves on phones. The iPhone and Android apps now keep a backup copy of your save, so it survives even if the phone clears the game's storage.
  • The website matches the apps. Every bonus button the apps have (2× speed, double rewards, revives, rerolls) now appears on the website too, and it's free on the web until ads are switched on there.

November 12–13, 2026 · Ads, privacy and security

  • Fixed a bug where closing a bonus video early could leave the game muted and stop further bonuses from loading.
  • Players in the EU, UK and Switzerland now get a proper consent choice, and can change it any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Security hardening across the site and the shop.
  • Cloud saves are safer when you play on two devices: a device that's behind now catches up instead of overwriting newer progress.
  • Clearer results when you retreat from a run (you keep everything you earned).

November 10–11, 2026 · The apps

  • Dead Signal on iPhone and Android. Public betas opened on TestFlight and the Google Play closed test.
  • A new, leaner and taller courier in both the 2D and 3D art styles.
  • The level-up screen now always fits without scrolling, even on small phones.
  • Auto-equip: better gear you find is equipped for you automatically.
  • Fresh app icon: the armored courier, a cracked glowing visor and the burning city behind.
  • A reroll on the level-up screen when you're out of rerolls (up to twice a run).

November 8–9, 2026 · Quality of life

  • New players now drop straight into a short training run.
  • Interrupted runs keep their rewards. If your phone rings mid-run, you don't lose anything.
  • Tap any gun or skill on the level-up screen to see its full stats.
  • The weapon gear slot is now a Gun Mod that upgrades your main gun.
  • Welcome-back rewards for returning players, and the option to install the game to your home screen.
  • Tutorial tips can be dismissed with one tap, and dismissing two mutes the rest for that run.

Earlier

Before November, Dead Signal grew from a single-sector prototype into a 20-sector campaign with bosses, gun evolutions, gear and talents, daily challenges, Endless mode, leaderboards, cloud saves and Ascension. Thanks to everyone who played those early builds and told us what was fun and what wasn't.
