Privacy Policy

Dead Signal · Last updated October 30, 2026

Dead Signal is a free browser game. We collect as little as we can. You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email, phone number or location.

What the game stores on your device

Your progress (sectors, gear, settings and similar game data) is saved in your browser's local storage so you can pick up where you left off. Clearing your browser data erases it, unless it's backed up with Cloud Save.

Cloud Save

To let you play on more than one device, the game backs up your save to our database (hosted by Supabase in the United States). This stores your game save, a random sync code, a random player ID and, if you set one, your in-game call sign. Anyone with your sync code can load that save, so keep it to yourself. We keep the last five versions of each save.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose Back up to Google Drive, you sign in with Google and the game stores one file containing your game save in your own Google Drive, in a hidden folder reserved for this game (the "app data" folder). The game asks only for access to that folder: it cannot see, open or change any of your other files. Google gives the game a temporary access key that expires after about an hour; we never see your Google password, and nothing about your Google account is sent to our servers. You can remove the game's access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which also deletes the hidden file. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Leaderboards

If you play the Daily Challenge or Endless mode, your score, run time, chosen courier, a random player ID and your call sign and crew code (if you set them) are stored so they can appear on the leaderboards. Call signs are visible to other players, so don't use your real name.

Gameplay statistics

To improve the game (which sectors are too hard, where players stop, whether ads work), the game sends anonymous gameplay events to our database: when a session starts, how long you played and which screen you left from, when a run starts and ends (sector, result, time survived, what defeated you, your gun, frame rate, whether you retreated), the first time each session you use a feature (such as a lobby tab or Power Up), unlocks, ads offered and watched, and game errors. Each event carries only the game's random player ID plus basic context: screen size, 2D or 3D graphics, touch or mouse, how many days since you started playing, the name of the site that linked you (not the full address) and a campaign tag if the link had one (like ?utm_source=reddit). We don't store your IP address, name or contact details, and we don't use this to show you ads. Stats are kept for up to 18 months.

You can turn this off any time in Settings → Share anonymous stats. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, stats start switched off.

Advertising

The game may offer optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one for an in-game reward). Ads are served by Google. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads and measure them, including ads based on your interests. Learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and change your ad settings at adssettings.google.com. Players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, we don't use third-party analytics or trackers, and we don't collect contact details.

Children

Dead Signal is intended for players 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will delete it.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset progress to erase your local save. To delete your cloud backups, leaderboard entries or gameplay statistics, contact us with your sync code or call sign.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: deadsignalsupportchannel@gmail.com

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
