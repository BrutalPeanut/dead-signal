About Dead Signal

Dead Signal is a free sci-fi zombie survival game you can play in any web browser, on a phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install. It's also in public beta on iPhone (TestFlight) and Android (Google Play).

The story is simple: a broadcast has turned the city's people into the infected, and you're the courier sent in to shut the signal down. Each run is a five-minute fight against a growing horde. Your weapons fire on their own, you choose upgrades as you level up, and you combine weapons with the right support upgrades to evolve them into something much stronger.

Who makes it?

Dead Signal is an independent game made by a single independent developer. There's no big studio behind it, which is why player feedback shapes so much of what changes from week to week. You can follow along on the devlog.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The whole game is free. Optional bonus videos give small extras like a second chance or double rewards, and you only ever see one if you tap to watch it. Nothing interrupts a run. There's also an optional shop for players who want to support development.

Does my progress save?

Yes. Progress saves automatically on your device. The game also backs it up to the cloud under a short Cloud Save code, which you can use to move your progress to another phone, browser or computer. On the website you can also back it up to your own Google Drive from Settings.

Why did my progress disappear in my browser?

Browsers delete a site's data if you clear your history or cookies, play in a private window, or (on iPhone Safari) don't visit for about a week. Adding Dead Signal to your home screen avoids the Safari limit, and your Cloud Save code always brings your progress back.

Can I play with a keyboard?

Yes: WASD or the arrow keys to move, and Escape to pause. You can also drag with the mouse, or switch to a fixed on-screen joystick in Settings.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts or passwords. Leaderboards use a call sign you choose, and the game's statistics are anonymous. See the privacy policy for exactly what's collected.

Will my beta progress carry into the full release?

Yes. Everyone who plays before launch is a Founder: your progress carries over, and you get a Founder's Pack when the full version launches.

How do I report a bug or suggest something?

Email deadsignalsupportchannel@gmail.com. Beta testers can also post in the tester group. Every message gets read.
