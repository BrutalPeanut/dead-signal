How to Play Dead Signal

The complete player guide · guns, evolutions, bosses and tips

Dead Signal is a free sci-fi zombie survival game that plays right in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install. A strange broadcast has turned the city's people into the infected, and you are the courier sent in to shut the signal down. Every run is a five-minute fight against an ever-growing horde: you move, your weapons fire on their own, and every few kills you choose an upgrade that changes how the rest of the run plays out.

Controls

On a phone or tablet, drag anywhere on the screen with one thumb to move. There are no fire buttons: every weapon aims and shoots by itself, so all you have to think about is where to stand.

On a computer, move with WASD or the arrow keys, or drag with the mouse. Press Escape to pause. The game also offers a fixed on-screen joystick in Settings if you prefer one.

How a run works

Each sector is a five-minute survival run. The infected come in waves that get thicker and tougher as the clock runs down, with a mid-run boss and a final boss to beat before the sector is cleared.

Infected drop experience crystals. Collect enough and you level up: the game pauses and offers you three upgrades. You can take a new weapon, level up one you already have, or add a support upgrade. You can carry several weapons at once, so a run is really about building a combination that works together.

If you go down, you may be offered a second chance for watching an optional ad, and the Second Wind perk gives you a free revive. You can also retreat at any time and keep everything you earned. Gold, scrap and gear you collect during a run carry back to the lobby whether you clear the sector or not.

Bosses

The Warden (Riot Unit 07, still on duty) is a heavily armored riot unit that charges straight at you. Keep moving sideways and let area weapons wear it down.

Hive Mother (she hears the signal loudest) is slow but spawns more infected around her. Clear the swarm first, then focus her.

The Broadcaster (still on the air) has the most health of any boss and hits hard. Bring sustained damage and a healing upgrade like Nano Medkit or Adrenal Pump.

Signal Colossus (the tower walks) is the slowest boss and the hardest-hitting. Never let it corner you against the edge of the screen.

The campaign

The campaign runs across 20 sectors in four kinds of places: the downtown streets, the biolab, the spaceport and, in Act II, the reactor that powers the signal. Each sector raises the infected's health and damage and pays out more gold. The sectors are: 1. Dead Air, 2. Static Row, 3. Biolab Ingress, 4. Containment, 5. Launch Gantry, 6. Night Shift, 7. Each sector shows a recommended power. If you're well below it, spend a few runs upgrading gear and talents before pushing on.

Getting stronger between runs

Gear. Runs and crates drop gear for six slots: your main gun plus armor pieces. Gear comes in rarities from common up, and you can level it with scrap. Better gear is equipped automatically when it drops, and you can always change it yourself in the lobby.

Talents. Spend gold on permanent boosts to Firepower, Conditioning (max HP), Toughness and Field Medic (healing). Your account level also unlocks free perks along the way, like extra rerolls, a revive per run and free level-ups at the start of each run.

Daily rewards and missions. Signing in, finishing missions and the Signal Pass all pay out gold, crate keys and gear.

Ascension. Once the campaign is behind you, Ascension restarts the climb with tougher infected in return for Signal Cores, which buy permanent bonuses.

Leaderboards. Daily challenge and campaign scores go on public leaderboards under a call sign you choose. No account needed.

Beginner tips

  • Keep moving. Standing still is how you get surrounded. Circle the horde and let it chase you into your weapons.
  • Grab crystals in sweeps. Loop back through the trail of crystals behind you instead of diving into the crowd for them. Magnet Core makes this much safer.
  • Pick a lane early. Choose one or two weapons to evolve and take their partner upgrades as soon as they're offered.
  • Mix ranges. Pair something that guards your body (Orbit Blades, Plasma Field) with something that hits far away (Arc Coil, Frag Launcher).
  • Take survival when it's offered late. Nano Medkit and Kinetic Plating keep a strong build alive through the final boss.
  • Stuck on a sector? Replay an earlier one for gold and gear. Power climbs quickly in the first few days.

Is Dead Signal free?

Yes. The whole game is free to play. Optional rewarded ads give small bonuses like a second chance or double rewards, and you only see one if you tap to watch it. Ads never interrupt a run. There's also an optional shop for players who want to support the game.

Can I put it on my phone's home screen?

Yes. Dead Signal can be added to your home screen like an app, and your progress saves on your device. Cloud Save lets you carry it to another device with a short code. Dead Signal is also coming to Google Play and the App Store.
