Dead Signal Strategy Guide

From your first run to Sector 20 and Ascension

Dead Signal looks simple: move, survive, pick upgrades. But the difference between dying at minute three and clearing a sector with time to spare almost always comes down to a handful of decisions. This guide covers the ones that matter most, in the order you'll run into them.

1. Plan your build around evolutions

Every weapon has a partner support upgrade. Get the weapon to its maximum level while you hold its partner and it evolves into a much stronger version. An evolved weapon is usually worth more than two un-evolved ones, so the most important habit to build is simple: when you take a weapon, start looking for its partner.

A good default plan for a run is one main evolution plus one backup. Take the first weapon that fits your plan, take its partner the first time it's offered, and keep levelling that pair before you spread into anything else. If the pieces for a second pair show up early, they make a great backup plan, but don't take five weapons at level one. Wide builds feel safe early and fall apart against the late waves.

2. Combinations that work well together

You want weapons that cover different jobs: something that protects your body, something that clears the crowd, and something that hits a boss hard.

  • Orbit Blades + Kinetic Plating → Halo Saw. The blades spin around you constantly and the plating cuts the damage you take. This is the most forgiving pair for new players, because it keeps the infected off you while you learn to move.
  • Plasma Field + Nano Medkit → Event Horizon. A burning aura that slows everything near you, paired with steady healing. Strong when you're surrounded, and great alongside a long-range weapon.
  • Arc Coil + Capacitor → Tesla Storm. Lightning chains through the crowd on its own. The Capacitor's cooldown reduction also speeds up everything else you carry, so it's never a wasted pick.
  • Frag Launcher + Volatile Fuel → Cluster Barrage. Big area damage for thick waves. Volatile Fuel's extra area helps every explosive and aura you own.
  • Disc Launcher + Magnet Core → Gravity Disc. The returning disc cuts lines through the horde, and Magnet Core's pickup range means you collect crystals without diving into danger.
  • Napalm + Thermal Gel → Inferno Grid. Burning pools that last longer and stack up. Excellent zone control when you circle the horde.
  • Plasma Cleaver + Adrenal Pump → Rift Reaper. A close-range sweep with healing from kills. Aggressive, but it sustains itself once the kill count climbs.

Your main gun (Vector SMG, Flamethrower, Heavy Machine Gun or Bazooka) has its own partner too. The SMG and machine gun shine against single big targets; the flamethrower and bazooka are better against packed waves.

3. Movement is your real armor

The infected come at you from every side, and the waves thicken toward the end of the five minutes. The safest pattern is to circle: move in a wide loop so the horde follows behind you in a line, which keeps them in your weapons' path and stops them from closing around you. Standing still, even for a few seconds, is how most runs end.

Collect crystals by sweeping back through the trail you've left behind, not by diving into the middle of the crowd.

4. Beating the bosses

The Warden charges in straight lines. Step sideways as it commits to a charge and punish it while it recovers. Hive Mother keeps spawning infected around herself, so clear the swarm with area weapons first, then focus her. The Broadcaster has the most health of any boss; this is where an evolved weapon and some healing pay off. Signal Colossus is slow but hits extremely hard. Keep your distance, never let it pin you against the edge of the screen, and let ranged damage do the work.

5. Getting stronger between runs

Each sector shows a recommended power. If you're well below it, don't keep throwing runs at it. Spend a few on earlier sectors, where clears are fast and rewards still add up, and put your gold and scrap into:

  • Gear upgrades. Levelling the gear you already wear is usually the cheapest power gain. Better drops are equipped automatically.
  • Talents. Firepower and Conditioning (max HP) are the best early buys.
  • Daily missions and the sign-in calendar. These pay crate keys and gold every day, and they add up quickly in your first week.

6. The late campaign and Ascension

The second half of the campaign moves into the reactor that powers the signal, and the infected get noticeably tougher. Players most often get stuck around Sector 12. If that happens, the answer is almost always power, not luck: a gear level or two and a talent upgrade make a bigger difference than another attempt with the same build. Once the campaign is behind you, Ascension restarts the climb with harder infected in return for Signal Cores, which buy permanent bonuses that carry across every future run.
